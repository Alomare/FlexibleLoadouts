# Changelog

Releases are named "Flexible Loadouts V&lt;n&gt;" (tag `v<n>`). Each section is that release's notes. Versions 1 and 2 were released as Stratagems Unleashed, 3 to 7 as Flexible Stratagems.

## V8 (unreleased)

- Renamed Flexible Loadouts. Mod managers update it in place, and your Clear Stratagems key stays bound.
- One file again: the Plus edition is gone.
- A red X in the corner of each filled stratagem slot empties that slot. It fades in and out like the game's own buttons, and the slot under it stops showing its hover frame.
- A REMOVE button at the top of your primary, secondary and grenade slots in the Hellpod loadout: checked, your Helldiver goes on missions without that weapon or grenades, while your loadout keeps them. The choice stays until you change it.
- The Clear Stratagems key is now **Action**, and works with a controller: it empties the hovered or selected stratagem slot (no longer all four), flips the REMOVE box of the hovered or selected weapon or grenade slot, pins the hovered or selected stratagem in the list, and on the booster slot rolls a random lineup. With a controller, the selected slot shows its X or REMOVE button. Your binding stays.
- Pinned stratagems: a pin on the stratagem under your cursor in the list. Pinned stratagems come first in their group (offensive, supply, defensive), and stay pinned between game sessions.
- Randomize Lineup: a small button between your 4th stratagem slot and the booster fills the four slots, one by one, with one of three random lineups from the stratagems you own. It hides when you own too few stratagems for a real choice.

## V7 (2026-10-07)

- Each release now has two files. Install one of them.
- `Flexible-Stratagems-7-Plus.zip`: pick the same stratagem more than once, up to all four slots, vehicles included. There is no Copies per Stratagem option.
- `Flexible-Stratagems-7.zip`: no duplicate stratagems and one vehicle of a kind, as in the game. Ready with fewer than four stratagems, the list kept open and Clear Stratagems are in both files.

## V6 (2026-10-06)

- Removed picking the same stratagem more than once, and the Copies per Stratagem option.
- You can still bring more than one vehicle of a kind, such as two different mechs.

## V5 (2026-10-05)

- Fixed: after readying with fewer than four stratagems, pressing Ready again right away was ignored for about two seconds. It now cancels (or readies) on the first press.
- Fixed: readying with fewer than four stratagems now plays the ready pose, for you and your squad.

## V4 (2026-10-05)

- Replaced the rulesets with a Mod Options Menu slider: Copies per Stratagem, from 2 to 4 (default 2). Vehicles are always included.
- Removed the cooldown on copies.
- You can ready up with fewer than four stratagems. The empty slots flash once, then you are ready.
- Replacing a stratagem in a full loadout keeps the list open on the next slot, where you scrolled.
- Added a Clear Stratagems key in Mod Bindings Menu: empties all four stratagem slots.
- Translated into every game language. Brazilian Portuguese, Latin American Spanish and Traditional Chinese now load (they showed in English before).

## V3 (2026-10-04)

- Renamed to Flexible Stratagems. Mod managers update it in place.
- Added rulesets in Mod Options Menu:
  - Off: the game's rules.
  - Less Restricted (default): up to two of each stratagem. Using one puts the other copy on a 10 s cooldown.
  - Unleashed: any stratagem any number of times (V2 behavior).
- The option follows the game language. Brazilian Portuguese included.
- If a game update moves only the copy cooldown code, only the copy cooldown turns off.

## V2 (2026-09-29)

- Addresses and offsets are found by code signatures, so the mod survives more game updates. If anything isn't found, the mod turns off and the loadout works as normal.

## V1

- First release (as Stratagems Unleashed): the Hellpod loadout takes the same stratagem more than once, vehicles included.
